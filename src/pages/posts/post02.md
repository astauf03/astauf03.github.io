## Project Summary

### This project analyzes active business licenses and Pennsylvania Liquor Control Board (PLCB)-liquor licenses within a portion of the East Passyunk neighborhood study area to characterize corridor-scale dining and nightlife activity using administrative data.

East Passyunk has always been known as a food tourist destination for vistors coming to Philadelphia. There is a specific area within the neighborhood (visible on the dashboard) with an intense concentration of food service activity. This analysis of business and liquor licenses in the neighborhood showcase a densely packed area ready to dine: food service activity comprises of about **30%** of all business licenses in the area. **14 of 21** liquor licenses are located on E Passyunk Avenue (within the study area) alone.
